# CRM User Guide — BrightLoop AI

1. **Sales Pipeline**: Drag-and-drop deals across 10 stages (`New Lead` -> `Won` / `Lost`) or switch to Table View.
2. **Lead 360° Drawer**: Click any deal card to inspect contact details, record multi-currency proposals, add notes, or jump to `/email-studio`.
3. **Tasks, Meetings & Proposals**: Manage follow-up tasks, schedule client meetings, and track BrightLoop service proposals.
