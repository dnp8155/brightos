# CRM AI Intelligence — BrightLoop AI

Powered by `src/packages/crm/services/conversation-intelligence.ts` (`POST /api/crm/conversation-intelligence`):
- Classifies replies into `Interested`, `Meeting Request`, `Request for Information`, `Not Interested`, `Out of Office`, or `Neutral`.
- Extracts objections and recommends next best actions without auto-sending messages.
